Subnautica is an underwater survival game that combines exploration, resource management, and a compelling story. The player must learn to adapt to an unknown alien environment on an ocean planet.

The Story

After the crash of the spaceship Aurora, the protagonist Ryley Robinson is stranded on the ocean planet 4546B. Left to fend for themselves, they begin a fight for survival against hunger, thirst, mysterious sea creatures, and an unknown virus wich has befallen the entire planet.

Gameplay Features

Research & Database

Your hand scanner is the most important tool to survive in this alien environment. By scanning plants, creatures, or alien tech, you unlock new blueprints and store everything in your PDA. It’s the best way understand how the planet works and to discover the secret it hides.

Crafting System

You won’t survive long without crafting: everything you scavenge from the ocean floor can be turned into useful gear at the fabricator. Whether you need to filter water, cook food, or build tools for your trips, this is where it happens. It is essential to progress and gear up for the challenges of the deeper ocean.

Base Building

Construct your own underwater sanctuary using the habitat builder to create a safe haven against the harsh pressures of the deep sea. Your base can be expanded with modular compartments, observation rooms, and functional areas like scanner rooms or massive alien aquariums. It serves as a necessary hub for resource storage, power generation, and specialized crafting stations to support your long-term exploration.

Exploration

Exploration is the heart of Subnautica, driving you to venture into deeper, more dangerous biomes to uncover the planet's secrets. You will need to upgrade your vehicle fleet, from the agile Seaglide to the versatile Seamoth and the massive, mobile Cyclops submarine. Each vehicle allows you to dive deeper, and withstand the extreme conditions of the uncharted ocean.
